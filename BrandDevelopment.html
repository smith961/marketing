Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between branding and marketing? expand_more

Branding is the definition of your identity—who you are and what you stand for. Marketing is the set of actions you take to communicate that identity and drive sales. Branding is your foundation; marketing is your megaphone.

How long does a brand development project take? expand_more

Typically, a full brand development cycle takes between 4 to 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of the research and the scope of assets required (logo, guidelines, website, etc.).

Do you provide ongoing brand management? expand_more

Yes, we offer retainer-based services to ensure your brand remains consistent across all touchpoints and evolves with the changing market dynamics in Nigeria.

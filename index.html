Understanding Digital Marketing in Lagos

What does a digital marketing agency in Lagos actually do? expand_more
A top-tier digital marketing agency in Lagos helps businesses navigate the unique Nigerian digital landscape. At Magnetize Marketing, we specialize in high-performance SEO, localized social media marketing, and data-driven lead generation tailored to Lagos’ competitive market dynamics.
How long does it take to see SEO results in the Nigerian market? expand_more
Typically, technical SEO improvements can show impact within 30-60 days. However, for highly competitive Lagos-based keywords, a sustainable and authoritative ranking strategy usually takes 4-6 months of consistent execution.
Why is Google Ads better for my Lagos business than Social Media? expand_more
Google Ads captures "active intent"—people searching specifically for your service. Social media is great for brand awareness. For most Lagos B2B and service companies, a hybrid approach works best to ensure both long-term brand equity and immediate lead flow.

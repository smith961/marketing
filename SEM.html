What is SEM?

Search Engine Marketing (SEM) is a digital marketing strategy used to increase the visibility of a website in search engine results pages (SERPs) primarily through paid advertising. In today's AI-augmented search landscape, SEM provides the immediate leverage businesses need to bypass organic competition.

By bidding on keywords that users in Lagos and across Nigeria enter when looking for certain products or services, Magnetize ensures your business appears exactly when intent is highest.

Google Ads FAQ

What are the SEM costs in Nigeria?

Costs vary significantly by industry. We manage budgets ranging from ₦250k to ₦20M+ monthly, ensuring each Naira is optimized for maximum conversion potential.

How fast will I see results?

Unlike SEO, SEM results are nearly instantaneous. Once your campaign is approved and goes live, your ads can start appearing for your target keywords within minutes.

GSO Common Questions

How is GSO different from traditional SEO? expand_more
Traditional SEO aims to get you on the first page of Google links. GSO aims to get your information processed and recommended by AI engines like ChatGPT as the definitive answer, often before the user even sees a list of links.
Is GSO relevant for small businesses in Lagos? expand_more
Absolutely. AI search is becoming the primary way mobile users in Nigeria find quick information. Being optimized for GSO ensures your local business is the one AI suggests for queries like "best marketing agency in Lagos."
When should we start optimizing for GSO? expand_more
Now. AI search models are training constantly. Establishing your business as a trusted entity early creates a data legacy that is hard for competitors to overcome later.

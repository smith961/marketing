Frequently Asked Questions

How does Digital PR differ from Traditional PR?

While Traditional PR focuses on print, TV, and radio for reach, Digital PR targets online publications, social media, and bloggers to gain high-quality backlinks and measurable online visibility.

What are the SEO benefits of Digital PR?

Digital PR is one of the most effective ways to build authority. High-authority backlinks from news sites signal to search engines that your brand is a trusted leader, boosting your rankings for competitive keywords.

Is Digital PR suitable for small businesses in Lagos?

Absolutely. We tailor strategies to focus on niche authority, ensuring that your local reputation becomes a springboard for wider market trust.
